# Contributor and troubleshooting experience

`CONTRIBUTING.md` documents prerequisites, layout, conventions, backend/frontend/SDK/database/docs
checks, security-sensitive change expectations, and feature proposals. `SECURITY.md` directs reports
to private channels without exposing a personal email. Structured bug/feature/security issue
templates and a PR checklist reinforce safe public collaboration.

The [troubleshooting guide](../troubleshooting.md) covers Docker/storage, PostgreSQL, Redis,
migrations, ports, routing, cookies, CSRF, rate limits, worker/providers, Prometheus, Grafana, OTel,
disk, Windows/WSL, and pnpm build policy. The [FAQ](../faq.md) answers common boundary questions.
