# Phase 7 review

Phase 7 delivers the approved deterministic Risk Engine and Policy Engine without beginning Gateway work. The pure domains implement the exact Phase 3 arithmetic and precedence contracts; persistence captures immutable profile/policy references and reconstructable explanations.

The application baseline is explicit rather than a hidden fallback. Management is tenant-safe, versioned, priority-constrained, audited, and restricted to Owner/Admin/Security Analyst mutations. The Playground and policy console make decisions and seeded policy origins visible.

Known boundaries are deliberate: the only risk profile is the immutable system V1 profile; Analyze expresses but does not enforce Gateway actions; review has no workflow; output enforcement awaits Phase 8; incidents/feedback automation remain future phases. False-positive feedback must never silently retrain detectors or mutate an active profile.

Acceptance evidence is recorded in tests and quality-gate results. Phase 8 has not started.
